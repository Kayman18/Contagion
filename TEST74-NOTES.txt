FM1 Virooos TEST74 - transpose modulation

Connects matrix destination 4, bringing the total to 73 connected sound
destinations. Ten retained presets configure non-neutral routes to it; this
includes one Random-source route, whose source remains zero as observed in
the original control path.

Retains fractional transpose and the original unbounded matrix contribution.
Oscillator key tracking scales its pitch effect, including oscillator 3's
shared oscillator-2 tracking. Filter tracking and pitch-following saturation
use the transposed pitch. Per-voice state avoids changing global transpose or
other voices. Phase is preserved and route removal restores base behavior.

Hardware examples (original zero-based programs):
C118 T Pot BC or G17 CrossQ BC: hold notes and move the mod wheel.
D44 GateRim HS and G68 NewSnareBC also use wheel-driven transpose.
C97 Rise up!SV uses LFO1; D67 Multasm HS uses LFO2.
Other assigned destinations may also move. Report CPU average/peak,
xruns/deadlines and any clicks; four-layer unison can provide heavier load.

Validation:
8192 comparisons against the captured original key-tracking function across
four key-tracking settings, all route amounts, four source levels and four
oscillator-3 modes. Maximum increment error 8.952e-8 cycles/sample.
ASan/UBSan checks verify phase preservation, voice isolation, route removal,
block-size invariance, pitch-following saturation, and equivalent filter
tracking for base transpose versus matrix transpose.
Full/reduced/rounded matrix routing, key-tracking, main-level and pitch
regressions passed. All 650 presets rendered finite/nonzero output over 4096
stereo frames each under ASan/UBSan. Unison/Analog Boost host scripts and
firmware CRC/package, executable-RAM and cache checks passed.
