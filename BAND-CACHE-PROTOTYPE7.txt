Test procedure:
1. Keep TEST74 available to restore. Load BAND7 EXP and let startup finish.
2. Hold a note until the wavetable component arrives; then repeat it.
3. Hold four notes; report CPU average/peak, xruns and deadlines.
4. Try a different octave and waveform; report the loading gap duration.
5. Check LFO shapes and preset changes still complete correctly.

Known experimental limitations:
New or stolen voices and changed pitch bands can temporarily silence the
wavetable component until reconstruction finishes. Same-band wave changes
may retain the old wave until ready. Saw/pulse components can continue.
There is no crossfade or startup oscillator-band prefetch yet. This is a
cache-integration diagnostic, not a replacement for the stable firmware.
