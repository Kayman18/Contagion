FM1 Virooos TEST71 - sub, noise and ring-mod level modulation

Connects matrix destinations 21, 23 and 90, bringing the total to 70 connected
sound destinations. Sixty-five retained presets configure non-neutral routes
to one or more of these controls.

Uses the original fractional source gain curves and control-step update
behavior. The captured original smooths output gains, not these source levels.
Per-voice cached gains replace audio-loop parameter/table lookups. Sub gain
retains its main oscillator volume scaling; noise and ring remain independent.
Ring gain retains the existing split between the two filter input paths.
Routes can enable a source whose base level is zero and mute it again.
Removing/rebinding the matrix restores the base gains without resetting noise
history or sub phase. Audible noise and noise FM share one generator sample.

Hardware examples (original zero-based programs), hold notes and move wheel:
D64 MinorityHS - sub level.
C13 Contra BC or C34 Freno BC - noise level.
C28 Etheral SV or D31 Dynette HS - ring-mod level.
Other assigned destinations may also move. Report CPU average/peak,
xruns/deadlines and any clicks. Four-layer unison can be used for heavier load.
