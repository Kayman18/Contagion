FM1 Virooos TEST65 - LFO rate and contour modulation

Adds five original matrix targets: LFO1 rate/contour (43/44), LFO2 rate/contour
(50/51), and LFO3 rate (57). There are now 59 connected destinations.
105 retained presets configure non-neutral routes to at least one of them.

Timing follows the captured source: read the current LFO values for the matrix,
apply per-voice rate/contour changes, then advance each enabled local LFO once.
Rate changes preserve phase, cycle count and completed one-shot state.
Removing the matrix restores base timing without retriggering.
Key tracking, note-on rate deviation and tempo clock divisions remain intact.
Clock divisions override raw rate modulation, matching the original.
LFO3 retains the source's fractional-rate behavior near its 127 endpoint.

These are per-voice routes in the original. Shared LFOs retain their independent
base rate and contour; a route does not turn a shared LFO into a local one.
The local LFO continues running while shared scope is selected.
Effective rate caches avoid recalculation when the value has not changed.
