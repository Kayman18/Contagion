FM1 Virooos TEST70 - oscillator key tracking modulation

Connects matrix destinations 10 and 19 (oscillator 1/2 key tracking), bringing
the total to 67 connected sound destinations. Three retained presets use them.

Preserves the original fractional tracking curve, clamped to 0..127 and
centered on note 60. Oscillator 2 tracking also affects oscillator 3, in slave
and independent modes. Tracking evaluates the current gliding pitch, pitch
bend/transpose and unison offset. Phase and pending BLEP state are preserved;
removing the matrix restores base tracking without retriggering notes.

Hardware examples (original zero-based programs):
C66 MoveMyMWBC: hold notes below/above middle C and move the mod wheel.
The tracking change is centered on MIDI note 60, so test away from that note.
C102 Sickly BC: keyboard position drives oscillator 2 tracking.
E52 GooHat M: LFO1 drives oscillator 2 tracking.
Report CPU average/peak, xruns/deadlines and clicks. Four-layer unison can be
used as a heavier test.

Validation:
49152 comparisons against the captured original keyfollow function, across
both targets, four base settings, all route amounts, four source levels,
three notes and four oscillator-3 modes. Maximum error 0.000536 cents and
2.484e-8 cycles/sample. ASan/UBSan checks cover phase/BLEP preservation, bands,
voice isolation, matrix removal and block-size invariance. Additional checks
cover fractional pitch, pitch offset and 1..4-layer unison.
Full/reduced/rounded matrix routing, 8192 detune cases, 46080 pitch cases and
23040 oscillator-3 cases passed. All 650 presets rendered finite/nonzero
output over 4096 stereo frames each under ASan/UBSan. Unison and Analog Boost
host scripts passed. Firmware CRC/package, hot-code and cache checks passed.
