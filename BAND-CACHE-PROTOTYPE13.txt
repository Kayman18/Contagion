BAND13 EXP — reuse ready tables across muted preset preparation

Effects deferred. Default worker budget remains1024. No new hardware results.

Ready cache entries now survive muted handoff reset during preset/LFO/wave
preparation. Required bands are reserved up front so preparing another wave
cannot evict a ready band needed later in the same preload. Newly completed
bands are also reserved until preparation finishes; then temporary pins are
released. Incomplete tables are discarded, never published or reused.

Only metadata is copied, not waveform samples. The audio mute acknowledgment
remains required before resetting ownership. Preset changes still panic/mute
as before; this is not seamless preset switching. Other startup/LFO work can
still cause a pause even when oscillator tables are reused.

Fixed bank-reinitialization recovery: preload progress now resets along with
the bank, avoiding resume from an obsolete partial index.

- changing one of three waves: five band reconstructions
- duplicate waves reuse existing entries
- required reference tables remain byte-exact
- changed selection during partial preload recovers

- first prepared audio request needs no reconstruction
- pending publication is safely discarded during acknowledged mute
